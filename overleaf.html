Overleaf & LaTeX Notes

When preparing for mathematics competitions, I often find myself randomly flipping through textbooks in hopes of finding X exact theorem I need. NSMLs were the trickiest--the was nothing exactly right. Everything was either too easy, too hard, or didn't cover the topic our coaches wanted us to practice. So, I decided to code my own notes in LaTeX, on the topics I wanted to cover. These notes are not meant to be completely comprehensive, but rather a quick reference for the most important theorems and techniques in each topic (if you were on the bus and needed a quick review). This way, I hope others won't have the struggle through the treasure hunt I went through every meet.
